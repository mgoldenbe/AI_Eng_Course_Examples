# Campus Library

The campus library is open Sunday through Thursday from 8:00 to 22:00. On Fridays it is open from 8:00 to 13:00. The library is closed on Saturdays and official holidays.

Students may borrow up to six books at a time. Most books can be kept for three weeks and renewed online unless another student has requested them. Late returns may result in a temporary suspension of borrowing privileges.

The second floor is reserved for quiet study. Group discussions should take place in the study rooms on the first floor. Study rooms can be reserved online for up to two hours at a time.
