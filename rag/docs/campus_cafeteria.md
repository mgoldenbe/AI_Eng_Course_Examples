# Campus Cafeteria

The campus cafeteria serves breakfast from 8:00 to 10:30 and lunch from 11:30 to 15:00. A smaller coffee counter remains open until 18:00 on weekdays.

The cafeteria offers meat, vegetarian, and vegan meals every day. Students with food allergies should ask a staff member before ordering, because ingredients may change and not every dish is prepared in an allergen-free environment.

Students can pay by credit card or with money loaded onto their student card. The student card receives a 10 percent discount on full meals, but the discount does not apply to drinks or snacks.
